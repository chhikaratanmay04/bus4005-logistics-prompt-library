# Prompt 7 – Complaint Triage

## Version 1

### Prompt
Classify this logistics customer complaint:

[CUSTOMER COMPLAINT]

### Example Input
My parcel arrived one day late and the outer box was crushed, although the item inside seems okay. I would like someone to call me.

### Test Output
This complaint relates to a late delivery and possible package damage. It should be reviewed by customer service and operations.

### Issues Identified
- No approved complaint categories are provided.
- No urgency rules are defined.
- The AI may classify the same complaint differently each time.
- There is no standard next-action field for staff.
