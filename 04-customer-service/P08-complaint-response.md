# Prompt 8 – Complaint Response

## Version 1

### Prompt
Reply to this logistics customer complaint:

[CUSTOMER COMPLAINT]

### Example Input
Complaint: My parcel arrived one day late and the outer box was crushed, although the item inside seems okay. I would like someone to call me.

### Test Output
We are sorry to hear about the late delivery and damaged outer packaging. We understand your concern and will look into the matter. A team member will contact you to discuss the issue.

### Issues Identified
- The prompt does not limit the response to approved information.
- The AI might promise refunds, compensation or actions that staff have not approved.
- It does not request useful shipment details.
- There is no staff-review label.
